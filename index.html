    The Vineyard edit

    What to pack for Martha's Vineyard

    Build around versatile layers: a flag knit for cool evenings, relaxed linen, white denim and comfortable shoes. In Oak Bluffs the look can move from a harbor walk past the gingerbread cottages to dinner. For the Martha's Vineyard African American Film Festival, think refined and comfortable. This is styling inspiration, not an event dress code.

    HBCU homecoming

    Collegiate, with context

    Morehouse College and Spelman College are cultural reference points for collegiate-inspired styling. Nothing here implies a school affiliation or endorsement; each institution's traditions are its own.
